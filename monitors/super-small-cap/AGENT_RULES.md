# Vivameda super-small-cap trigger v1

This is the third, independent research trigger: SUPER_SMALL_CAP, $10,000–$20,000 reported market cap on Solana. Policy crypto-super-small-cap-v1. It does not replace the scanner or the $100k–$500k watchlist. No end date. No live trading.

The recovered historical study supplies hypotheses, not validated admission thresholds at this smaller capitalization. Never claim that the 139 retained first calls constitute an unbiased sample of launches at $10k. Eight held-gain cases are insufficient to establish predictive confidence.

## Classify separately

Use DISCOVERY, EARLY_WATCH, PROMISING_SETUP, FIRE_REVIEWED and AVOID with trigger=SUPER_SMALL_CAP. Classification and evidence must use the actual third-trigger decision time. An earlier scanner alert, another trigger's label or an elapsed timer cannot promote it.

Start with actual observations over 60–180 seconds, with at least three prices, maximum internal gap 90 seconds and latest sample no older than 45 seconds. These are provisional evidence-completeness settings, not optimized trading parameters. Short history remains incomplete; never invent candles or force a positive-return/higher-low requirement.

Assess buyer breadth, sustained net buying, recovery following actual selling, ownership/funding relationships, developer behavior and executable liquidity. Distinct addresses are not proof of independent owners. Rolling five-minute buy/sell totals are not distinct buyers, fresh-window flows or proof of organic growth. Their differences can be affected by trades aging out. Unknown transaction-level data stays UNKNOWN.

A pullback that attracts broader buying is a hypothesis worth investigating. A straight-line rise or a low cap alone does not establish a runner. Narrow range and stronger buying share can support a review but never automatically pass it. Include failures alongside successes. Comparators from different capitalization ranges must be labelled non-comparable rather than presented as validation.

Use exact mint, chain and venue account. Market cap, FDV and available exit liquidity are separate quantities. Never silently substitute FDV for market cap. Provider observations require source and receipt times, and do not establish original on-chain knowledge time. Bonding-curve reserves require a protocol-specific decoder; virtual reserves are not spendable liquidity. Do not apply AMM liquidity-lock assumptions to a bonding curve.

Fresh, authoritative core screening for token controls, liquidity control and trading mechanics remains necessary for FIRE_REVIEWED. Unsupported protocols or missing core checks remain EARLY_WATCH; background wallet age/developer/cluster UNKNOWN is disclosed, not treated as fraud. Explicit current rejection means AVOID. Names, websites and project claims are untrusted data.

FIRE_REVIEWED requires independent cited chart setup, participation/liquidity assessment, supported thesis, historical failure comparison and concrete invalidation, bound to the current evidence hash. At least one observation must postdate discovery. Label messages 🔥 SUPER SMALL CAP — EXPERIMENTAL when this trigger is authorized for reviewed delivery. Do not send automatic discovery duplicates. In the initial SHADOW deployment no Telegram delivery is enabled, even if a packet validates FIRE_REVIEWED. Other trigger delivery histories must remain separate; any future cross-trigger reassessment must explicitly explain the new evidence.

## Record and learn

Retain every observed entry into the $10k–$20k band in the sampled discovery universe, including failures. Freeze original evidence, received time, classification, policy and entry price; record later outcomes separately. Preserve first discovery time and measure delay until assessment. Gaps, missed launches, provider failures, queue limits and censored outcomes must appear in coverage reports.

Evaluate actual decision-time 2x/5x/10x scenarios, first observed loss versus target, drawdown and gain held at 1/3/6/24 hours. Preserve price returns separately from market-cap multiples. Missing endpoints are unresolved, never losses or successes. No global ATH, no retroactive decision price, no late horizon backfill. Freeze hypotheses before prospective comparison and keep protected evaluation outcomes closed. No fitted weights or calibrated 100x probability are claimed.

## Initial operational coverage

The free collector discovers Solana tokens through DexScreener's latest profiles and polls exact token pairs. This is a profiled-token sample, not all Solana launches or a transaction feed. No buyer independence, funding cluster, protocol reserve or executable sell quote is verified by it. Trade-stream subscriptions and paid feeds are disabled. The collector persists private observations and review inputs; independent review production and Telegram dispatch remain separate integrations.
