# Historical call audit findings — 11 October 2026

The owner-run audit completed at 11:18:47 UTC. It reviewed 159 ordinary ALERT events, comprising 139 first-call tokens and 20 repeats. There were 78 eligible observed paths and 61 missing/incomplete cases. Ninety-seven ALERT events were excluded because their mints belong to the protected prediction-ledger cohort. No separate fire deliveries entered the audited cohort. This is not a complete audit of every token ever mentioned or of recent fire calls.

These are local market-cap observations, not price candles, executable exits or net trading profits. Peak multiples, gains held, and first observed target-versus-loss order are different outcomes. A brief spike cannot establish a sustained winner.

These differences do not establish a rule: winners are few, lookback lengths differ, historical knowledge times and policy versions are unverified, and buying counts are not proof of unique organic buyers. Shorter observation windows can make ranges look narrower. One-hour winners had higher volume acceleration than one-hour failures, while 24-hour winners had lower acceleration; a generic volume rule would mix different objectives.

## Missing charts and recovery

All 159 audited events lacked enough retained pre-call price samples to reconstruct the setup. The live scanner's 48-hour snapshot cleanup explains the absence of these older records. It does not establish that every archived copy is gone. An existing passive snapshot archive and scoped crypto backups were discovered, but the engineering account cannot read them.

A separate fixed-path owner recovery command now queries only snapshots in existing direct SQLite sources, for the already-audited non-protected mints and pre-call windows. It re-checks current protected identifiers, rejects conflicting samples rather than silently choosing a source, and does not decompress backup archives. No production service, source, threshold, permission, notification state or model is changed.

Recovery script: recover_charts.py. Tests: test_recover_charts.py. Fourteen audit/recovery tests passed locally and on the engineering server. Production scanner and learning V2 remain active. No fitted rule or trained weights were created, and the live fire relay still has its previous behavior.

One conflicting timestamp was removed; one compressed backup was not opened. The fixed-window study then found 132 usable first-call charts and 74 with complete 24-hour outcomes.
