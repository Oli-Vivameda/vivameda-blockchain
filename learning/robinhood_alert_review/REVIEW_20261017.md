PRELIMINARY retrospective research. No production deployment, trading, model fit or threshold change.

1. Question: audit every alert in the owner-supplied Robinhood Stampede Telegram export, separate supported outcomes from missing evidence, and identify the actual sender requiring chart links.
2. Population: 4,585 STAMPEDE WATCH alerts, 4,425 distinct chain-4663 contracts, 12 September–17 October 2026. Exclude one /start and one connection test. Retain 160 follow-ups; earliest delivery per contract is the primary unit. All exported symbols were unknown.
3. Method freeze: a written/hashed audit plan preceded market lookup and outcome calculation. Candle details and a delivery-time correction were written before reviewing labels. No prior Git preregistration commit exists; this is exploratory reconstruction, not prospective validation. Source HTML SHA256: cb88e32d78f0b09f3a703258d0a0f6522ee19e46733d8f26a2198a8e07d91faf.
4. Reference: first completed one-minute USD candle opening at/after Telegram delivery and closing within 180 seconds. Export dates were interpreted Asia/Nicosia UTC+03, consistent with all 4,585 event-to-message lags (0–122 seconds, median 9). The export itself lacks an explicit timezone offset. Never credit an earlier event-time entry.
5. Outcomes: independent witnessed >=2x high and <=0.3x low within 24 hours, excluding the reference candle's extrema. Both flags may occur; their joint presence does not establish their order. No interpolation, lifetime ATH label or assumption of an executable fill. Missing intervals cannot establish no-hit/failure.
6. Data and exclusions: all 4,425 contracts queried successfully in 148 public DEX Screener batches. 136 currently have a matching base-token pool; 4,289 have none. Absent listing is not failure. Historical public GeckoTerminal responses retained for 68 pools that existed near the embedded event time. 66 usable reconstructed references; no original pool was pinned in the messages. No protected scanner database, frozen ledger/outcomes, model probabilities, keys or credentials were read. No private cases/provider responses are published.
7. Results: 41 witnessed 2x; 38 witnessed a >=70% decline; 17 witnessed both. Mutually exclusive evidence groups: 24 with 2x only witnessed, 21 with severe decline only witnessed, 17 with both, 4,363 unresolved. “Only witnessed” does not prove the other event never occurred. Just two histories pass the 24-hour coverage test. All-alert win rate remains unknown. Current listings and reference availability create strong selection bias.
