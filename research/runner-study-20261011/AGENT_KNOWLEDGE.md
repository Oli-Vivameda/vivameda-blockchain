# Runner research knowledge v1 — provisional

Created 11 October 2026. Research window: 11 October 2024 to 11 October 2026, Cyprus time. This pack is preliminary source research, not a completed top-100 chart study. No weights, validated probabilities, production policy or automatic FIRE eligibility are supplied.

## What the agent must distinguish

1. Discovery is an observation, not a conviction upgrade. A scanner alert, low cap, passed token controls, profile boost or high transaction count alone never establishes a runner thesis. Do not echo discovery with FIRE. A separate decision needs independently assessed current evidence and an explicit reason it adds information.
2. Distinguish attention, ownership and executable demand. Attention is people discussing something; ownership is wallets holding supply; demand is buyers committing capital at prices they can also exit. None can substitute for another. Unique wallets do not prove independent people; clusters and common funding can explain apparent breadth.
3. Distinguish the initial launch, first pullback, later reclaim and exchange-listing phase. A later listing can be a new catalyst but cannot be credited to a launch-time detector. Futures listing does not establish spot availability. The observed publication time and event time are separate fields.
4. A narrative source establishes what was said, not that it caused the price change. Use CAUSAL_UNTESTED for proposed explanations. A matching price response can support an association, not isolate the cause from market beta, liquidity or concurrent events.
5. Biggest price excursion, held return and realizable profit are different outcomes. Keep the full path: peak, endpoint, drawdown, time to peak, time below entry, liquidity, supply continuity and exit evidence. A token can be both a spectacular spike and a poor trade for a later buyer.

## Audience assessment

Ask who the identifiable audience is, where it already gathers, what makes the symbol easy to repeat, and why people outside the promoter's own account would care. Record original URL, publication time, retrieval time, archive status and observed metrics at the decision time. Today's followers or retrospective articles cannot reconstruct early adoption.

Separate existing cultural reach from token-community growth. Record whether independent accounts are creating new content, whether discussion spreads across communities, and whether participation continues after the initial post. These are hypotheses for testing, not proven winning features. Paid boosts, copy-pasted comments, coordinated accounts and unidentified promoters remain uncertainty, not automatic rejection. Missing data stays UNKNOWN.

Do not require institutional utility for a cultural token; do not mistake lack of utility for a bullish thesis either. An authentic famous sponsor can attract attention while concentrated wallets extract value. Authenticity and distribution safety are separate checks.

## Price-action assessment to test

Evaluate structure relative to token age and launch mechanism. Use actual timestamped trades or candles; do not draw intraminute candles from snapshots or infer high/low ordering within a candle.

- Developing base: range stabilizes while price stops making persistent new lows. Ask whether participation and quote-side liquidity persist. Flat price with inactive trading is not demonstrated accumulation.
- Pullback support: the first burst is followed by a drawdown, then selling pressure appears to weaken while demand returns. A higher low is one possible observation, not a compulsory veto or guarantee.
- Reclaim: price returns above a previously observed level and remains there in subsequent observations. A single wick or tiny trade is insufficient to establish a held reclaim.
- Continuation: further advances occur with continued participation and liquidity. Rising price alongside falling quote reserves or concentrated selling weakens the thesis.
- Exhaustion/extraction: repeated failed recoveries, thinning liquidity, concentrated exits or loss of independent participation warrant downgrading. A new core REJECT overrides enthusiasm.

Measure these quantities; do not manufacture thresholds: return over non-overlapping windows; high/low range; realized volatility; drawdown and recovery duration; amount and concentration of signed buy/sell flow; new independently funded buyers; repeat-buyer share; holder retention; quote-reserve changes; usable sell quotes at the intended size. Compare both successful and failed cases from the same cap/age/venue/market regime.

## Participation and manipulation

Rolling five-minute counts overlap; do not add them across samples or label them organic buyer growth. More trades can reflect repeated wallets, bundles or wash-like activity. A cluster is evidence of linkage, not proof of criminal intent. Exclude documented AMM/program/escrow addresses from ownership calculations and report excluded amounts and unresolved supply. Unknown ownership is not PASS.

Track quote-side reserves and LP changes, not only a reported USD liquidity number. LIBRA is a source-reported example in which liquidity extraction matters alongside price and selling activity. Retrospective investigator claims require original dated evidence before use as an early signal. A report published in 2026 cannot be smuggled into a 2025 decision packet.

## How to classify with this knowledge

Retain the existing labels and core screening policy. This pack cannot promote a token by itself.

- DISCOVERY: token or cap-band entry observed; thesis not assessed.
- EARLY_WATCH: interesting evidence or missing/incomplete current evidence; explain the missing piece.
- PROMISING_SETUP: a defensible structure hypothesis with cited participation/audience evidence; explicitly disclose gaps and invalidation. Do not declare a validated edge.
- FIRE_REVIEWED: only the existing independently reviewed, identity-bound, fresh-evidence dispatcher may decide eligibility. Supply the strongest supporting observation, strongest counterevidence, comparable winner and failure, and why this decision adds information beyond discovery. Source stories or this pack alone cannot satisfy chart or comparator gates.
- AVOID: actual current rejection or a substantiated adverse finding under policy; do not use ignorance as certainty.

The review output should say: setup and phase; audience evidence and its time; actual demand evidence; supply/liquidity risks; relevant comparison and differences; invalidation; missing evidence. No invented percentage confidence, guaranteed 100x, backdated entry credit or trading instruction.

## Learning discipline

Do not say the agent learned from 100 charts until 100 verified ranking records and their early chart packets actually exist. Currently there are zero verified ranked runners and zero independently audited early chart setups in this study. Eleven source-case cards are preliminary research only. Keep late facts out of decision features, hash evidence before comparing outcomes, preserve failures and delistings, and test on a later untouched chronological cohort grouped by token/operator where linkage is known. No weights or production thresholds change until that evidence exists.
