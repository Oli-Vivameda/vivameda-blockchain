# Two-year runner study protocol

Status: data coverage blocked; source research started. Target: 100 ranked Solana small-cap runners plus failed lookalikes, not an arbitrary list of 100 famous coins. The owner asked for the biggest 100 in the last 24 months. The Solana scope follows the active small-cap project; a global cross-chain ranking would be a separate population.

Window: 2024-10-10T21:00:00Z through 2026-10-11T18:09:07Z (local start of 11 October 2024 through request time). No event after the request may enter outcomes. No global all-time-high label. No protected Vivameda pilot outcomes are accessed or reused.

## Universe and ranking

Acquire the complete discoverable launch/mint/pool universe over the window, including dead pools, delisted tokens and losers. Publish provider inclusion rules and missing counts. A current market-cap list, a list of exchange listings or a researcher-curated dataset does not establish a complete two-year universe. An export can support only its explicitly documented subset.

Bind chain, mint, canonical pool, quote token, decimals, supply provenance, launch/migration events and raw-response hashes. Keep pool migrations and token redenominations explicit. FDV is not market cap. Null identity/supply/liquidity never becomes a fabricated value.

Primary early-entry cohorts: first verified observed crossing into $10k–$20k and separately $100k–$500k market cap. Do not select the cheapest later point. Freeze the first qualifying observation timestamp and price, then compute early features only from evidence available by that decision time. If there is no sampled crossing, mark unavailable rather than interpolate. A token first observed above the band is not credited with a hypothetical tiny-cap entry.

Suggested primary ranking, to freeze before quantitative analysis: highest sampled forward price multiple from that first observed entry over the next seven days, restricted to the request window. Require actual prices with trade provenance and sustained-price confirmation; report raw sampled peaks separately. The ranking is descriptive and hindsight-selected, not a strategy win rate. A full-period ranking across all cryptocurrencies cannot be claimed from these restricted cap cohorts.

Require matched data resolution for comparisons and a published continuity rule. Store price path endpoints at 1h, 3h, 6h, 24h and 7d, peak and trough, endpoint multiple, drawdown, time to peak, and post-peak collapse. Offline reconstructed histories must be labelled RETROSPECTIVE_RECONSTRUCTION; they are not prospective captures. Original on-chain event time and our current retrieval time are distinct. Do not backfill the live pilot.

Historical fills are unknown unless archived routes/quotes or pool-state reconstruction actually establish executable results. At $300 size, fees, slippage and gas can materially affect tiny-cap results. Do not translate price multiples into realizable profit or 100x probability.

## Early chart and social packets

Freeze snapshots at first eligible band, then 1, 3, 5, 15 and 60 minutes when available. A later snapshot is a later decision, not credit to the first call. Features use only times <= that snapshot. Future known-event schedules require an original announcement already published by then; post-event listing accounts are not launch-time features.

Measure audience growth, original content, cross-community diffusion, independent wallet participation, buyer retention, flow concentration, funding clusters, supply and quote-side liquidity. Current social pages are background only; reconstruct contemporaneous follower/post statistics from dated archives or mark UNKNOWN. Record promotion spend when known. Search-engine snippets may establish a research lead; exact claim-time archival evidence is required for replay.

For causality, build a timeline: original attention event, first token mention, first independently sourced demand, migration, initial burst, pullback, later catalyst, and exchange listings. Grade explanations as observed event / measured association / untested mechanism. Do not call a later explanatory story a proven cause.

## Controls and validation

For each runner, select three failures or non-runners from the same week, chain, cap band, age, venue and measured pre-decision liquidity. Match without looking at candidate setup features that are to be evaluated. Avoid known shared operators between development and validation groups. Curated celebrity failures are useful caution cases but not statistically matched controls.

Split chronologically into development and untouched validation cohorts; do not repeatedly tune against validation. Compare a simple cap/liquidity/age/momentum baseline with added audience/distribution features. Publish sample coverage, false positives, precision, recall, calibration if predicting probabilities, and results by market regime. One memorable winner is not validation. Retain all sampled entrants for denominators.

No fitted weights, invented cutoffs or automatic FIRE upgrade are permitted from the eleven preliminary source cards. A complete top-100 ranked sample and inspected early packets are minimum study evidence, not sufficient proof of a profitable edge by themselves.

## Data options checked

CoinGecko connected max chart: tested SOL; returned 120 downsampled prices across 2020–2026 with only start/end timestamps, no per-point timestamps, trade volumes or early-pool history. It is unsuitable for this replay; do not synthesize point dates.

CoinGecko's official guide documents six months for free on-chain OHLCV and full history from 2021 for paid on-chain plans. Its spot history plan limits differ. Bitquery documents Solana aggregate archive from June 2024, but archive/combined queries require an archive pack; archive completeness and raw-high outliers are noted limitations. No archive subscription has been purchased or approved in this study.

MELT (formerly MemeTrans) supplies a researcher-curated Solana launch dataset with pre/post-migration traces. It is not the full requested two-year top-100 universe. Its repository states CC BY-NC 4.0; no code or dataset was imported into the commercial agent. Untrusted pickle files must not be deserialized as a download convenience.

Required unblock: an existing licensed archive export or an authorized archive service providing complete mint/pool discovery, timestamped trade/OHLCV history and supply/liquidity provenance for this window. Archive pricing and eligibility need verification before any purchase. Existing public case facts and provisional knowledge can be retained while archive access remains blocked.
